📖 玩法及经济说明

一、开始之前：钱怎么进、怎么出

  • 所有抽卡、养成、对战、下注，扣的都是合约内余额，不是钱包余额。路径：钱包 TKCC → approve 授权 → deposit 充值 → 合约内余额。
  • 所以下注时钱包里看不到 TKCC 变动，这是正常现象，不是没扣钱。
  • 赢的钱先进入合约内余额，必须点「领取奖励」才提回钱包。
  • 领奖交易会同时产生一条销毁记录。销毁量不是你的到账金额，请以到账提示 / transfer 事件为准。
  • 原生 PAXI 永不销毁；只有 TKCC 会销毁。销毁是直接减少总供应，不转入任何地址。
  • 运营收款地址（唯一）：paxi194kpjqhyz7re2g749lc2030cgeg4sql5ldvyem
  • 链上金额精度 6 位：1 TKCC = 1,000,000。
  • 无感模式：由会话地址代为签名，gas 仍由主钱包通过 feegrant 支付，约 0.03 PAXI / 笔。

1. Before you start: how money moves

  • All draws, upgrades, battles and bets are paid from your in-contract balance, not your wallet. Flow: wallet TKCC → approve → deposit → in-contract balance.
  • That's why your wallet shows no TKCC change when you bet — this is normal, the fee was still charged.
  • Winnings land in your in-contract balance first. You must tap "Claim" to move them back to your wallet.
  • A claim transaction also emits a burn entry. The burn amount is NOT what you receive — trust the claim toast / the transfer event instead.
  • Native PAXI is never burned; only TKCC is. Burning reduces total supply directly and sends to no address.
  • Treasury address (the only one): paxi194kpjqhyz7re2g749lc2030cgeg4sql5ldvyem
  • On-chain precision is 6 decimals: 1 TKCC = 1,000,000.
  • Seamless mode: a session address signs for you; gas is still paid by your main wallet via feegrant, ~0.03 PAXI per tx.
